What people come back for

    Arrachera

    Marinated skirt steak, grilled and sliced, served with rice, beans and tortillas.

    Shrimp Chipotle

Good to know

How many Añejo locations are there?

11, in Central Florida (Hunters Creek, Winter Park, Daytona Beach) and Northeast Florida (Riverside, San Marco, St. Johns, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Parkway, Yulee, Middleburg).

Can I order online?

Yes, at most locations. Open your location page and tap Order online. Hunters Creek takes pickup orders by phone.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes. The cauliflower tacos are vegetarian, and the guacamole is made to order at every location.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Añejo gift cards are available at the restaurants.
